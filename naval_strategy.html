📖 RULES
Board: 10x10. Standard chess rules for King, Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight, Pawn.

T-Sub (Red/Green): up to 4 squares forward/backward, then 1 left or right. Cannot jump.

V-Sub (Blue/Grey): up to 3 diagonal squares per arm (all 4 diagonal directions). Cannot jump.

Subs start flanking King & Queen. Win by capturing enemy King.
